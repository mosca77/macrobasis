- **CBO deficit data:** FY2026 deficit through nine months is **$1.4 trillion**, ~$35B ahead of the same point in FY2025; June alone ran a **$120-126B** monthly deficit. CRFB projects the full-year deficit will likely exceed **$2 trillion**, reversing last year's tariff-revenue-aided improvement.
- **$95B Iran-war reconciliation supplemental — stalled:** House passed a FY2027 budget resolution unlocking this package 216-214 on July 22 ($73B defense/intel tied to the Iran war, $12B ag aid, $10B election security). Senate Majority Leader Thune says the Senate "lacks votes"; may instead fold it into fall funding leverage. Critics say it adds ~$130B to the debt with zero offsets.
- **Government funding:** House passed a continuing resolution (220-205, Jul 21) extending FY2027 funding through **December 4**. Senate path needs 60 votes; mood described as "not negative" as of July 22, though Iran-related defense fights complicate talks. Two shutdowns already occurred earlier this Congress (43 days Oct-Nov 2025; 76-day partial DHS shutdown Feb-Apr 2026).
- **Tariff regime transition (live):** The 10% Section 122 global baseline tariff expired at its 150-day statutory limit on **July 24**; USTR's Section 301 replacement took effect the same day — a two-tier 10%/12.5% structure covering ~99.4% of imports. Layered on top: Brazil's 25% Section 301 tariff (Jul 22, with 1,600+ exemptions), Canada's new 50% tariffs on autos/alcohol/dairy (signed Jul 20, effective Aug 19, applying even to USMCA-qualifying goods), and the EU's 15% trade-deal ceiling (effective Jul 1).
- **IEEPA tariff refunds — a fiscal drag:** Following February's Supreme Court ruling striking down IEEPA tariffs, CBP had repaid **$86.3B** of a ~$166B disputed pool as of July 10; June alone saw $49.1B in refunds vs. only $23.6B in new tariff revenue that month — a net cash outflow for the tariff program this fiscal year.
- **Russia-sanctions bill / tariff-authority fight:** The bipartisan "Graham Act" (Russia secondary-tariff sanctions authority, routed through Congress/USTR to survive the IEEPA ruling) cleared a procedural vote around July 28; Democrats (Warren, Wyden) split over ceding any tariff authority to the executive.
- **Treasury long-end pricing:** 30-year yield at 5.06% (Jul 18-19), 30-year TIPS yield at 2.87-2.91% — highest since TIPS were reintroduced in 2010 — a market-based referendum on the deficit/issuance trajectory above.
- **Deregulation:** CFPB released its 2026 deregulatory rulemaking agenda in July; the GENIUS Act (stablecoin law) hit its one-year mark (Jul 19) with FDIC/OCC still finalizing implementing rules.
- **Background:** Moody's downgraded the US to Aa1 in May 2025; Fitch affirmed AA+/stable (last action Aug 2025) while flagging debt/GDP rising to 127% by 2027. No new rating action today, but CRFB's >$2T deficit projection raises the odds one resurfaces. Next binding debt-ceiling "X-date" not expected until winter-to-mid-2027.
