## MARKET-WIDE (FRONT PAGE)

**1. US-Japan conduct first joint yen intervention in years — dollar drops broadly**
Treasury and Japan's Ministry of Finance confirmed a coordinated intervention Aug 3 to buy yen after USD/JPY hit a 40-year high above ¥163. Japan's FM Katayama confirmed the action; Trump confirmed US involvement. Unusually, the NY Fed reportedly funded its side by selling **euros** rather than dollars — a mechanic former Treasury officials (Edwin Truman, Mark Sobel, Robin Brooks) called "weird" and "unwise," since it dilutes the standard signaling value of intervention and creates EUR spillover risk. USD/JPY fell to ~¥156-157 (from >¥163); DXY dropped to its lowest since mid-June (~99.5), worst weekly performance in three months. Bessent said the US "will not hesitate to participate in further joint intervention." *Note: sourcing conflicts on whether this is the first joint US-Japan intervention since 1998 (NPR, Fortune) or since 2011 (Al Jazeera, other coverage) — could not resolve with remaining search budget; treat "first in X years" framing with that caveat.*
