Core dispute is Hormuz control: Iran cites the MOU as giving it management rights over the strait; Trump claims US control and threatened to formally declare Hormuz US territory "pretty soon" (rejected by Iran as "delusional"); Trump also threatened to bomb Oman if it "gets in the way." **New Aug 19 escalation**: the UAE said two ballistic missiles were launched from Iran toward its maritime traffic (both fell into the sea); Iran denied it, calling the claim a "false flag." The UAE responded by halting all trade, commercial and financial transactions with Iran indefinitely — the first direct UAE-Iran confrontation of this war. Hormuz traffic remains near-frozen (roughly 1 vessel transited Aug 9 vs ~73/day pre-war) despite Trump's "control" claims; a ~500 sq mile oil spill off Oman from a stranded tanker has spread to the Omani mainland (Tier 1, Al Jazeera/Bloomberg/CNBC: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/19/uae-imposes-indefinite-trade-embargo-on-iran-over-alleged-missile-attacks; https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/trump-takes-hard-line-on-iran-as-hormuz-standoff-drags-on).

### Russia-Ukraine
No ceasefire; war remains active and intensified in-window. Ukraine's Defense Intelligence (Aug 18) said Russia shows no sign of readiness for ceasefire talks and no peace deal should be expected by year-end, with Moscow focused on seizing all of Donetsk and Luhansk. **Aug 12 Novorossiysk strike** (drone/Neptune-missile/sea-drone operation) damaged at least 4 Russian warships, halted two major grain terminals, and killed 3 civilians — Zelensky called it a "unique operation." **Aug 18**: a Russian missile strike killed 10 people in a Kharkiv-region village; Ukraine answered with one of its largest drone assaults of the war (~620 drones at Moscow region), hitting a Wildberries warehouse and causing widespread power outages in occupied Crimea. Ground war continues grinding near Chasiv Yar/Druzhkivka in Donetsk. The Senate's 86-11 Russia/Iran sanctions bill remains stalled in the House pending a floor vote (recess until Aug 31) (Tier 1, Al Jazeera/Kyiv Independent/Newsweek: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/18/russia-says-more-than-600-drones-targeted-moscow-region; https://kyivindependent.com/drone-attacks-hit-russias-novorossiysk-occupied-crimea/).

### CUSMA / Canada-US Tariffs
**Resolved for now at the deadline.** The 50% tariffs on ~US$20bn of Canadian goods (autos, alcohol, dairy, cement, wood, chemicals, minerals, textiles) were set to take effect just after midnight Aug 19; Trump instead paused implementation for three days (to end of day Friday), posting that Canada and the US "have a DEAL" subject to finalizing documents, and referencing a possible Keystone XL revival. Carney: "substantial progress has been made, although there is important work still to be done"; Carney and Trump spoke by phone Aug 18 ahead of the deadline. Net: tariffs did not take effect as of report time; the new soft deadline is end-of-day Aug 21, with dairy, autos, aluminum and lumber still contested (Tier 1, CBC/NPR/Axios: https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/new-trump-tariffs-deadline-9.7311125; https://www.axios.com/2026/08/19/trump-canada-tariffs-50-percent-postponed).
