## Divergence list (carried into the text, not dropped)
1. **Breakevens anchored at 2.28% while September hike odds nearly doubled.** The long end sold off on term premium and issuance, not on inflation compensation. This is why the inflation call is "held".
2. **Equal-weight S&P 500 at an all-time high in the same week the cap-weighted index fell and GDP missed.** Breadth is not confirming the growth slowdown.
3. **Brent finished the week about 6% lower despite a direct Iranian missile attack on a US base and the collapse of the Hormuz talks.** The oil market is discounting escalation headlines. Caps the energy and geopolitics readings.
4. **Gold crested $4,100 but remains roughly 26% below its 29 January record of $5,597, and Bitcoin is down 46% year on year.** The debasement complex is not confirming as a bloc.
5. **Canadian gold miners fell while gold rose.** Unresolved; reported as an observation, not explained.
6. **VIX only reached about 20.7 on the worst equity session since April 2025.** Volatility is not confirming the stress.
7. **Credit is a gap, not a confirmation.** The last hard HY OAS print is 277bp from 23 July. Nvidia CDS is the only fresh credit signal and it widened.
