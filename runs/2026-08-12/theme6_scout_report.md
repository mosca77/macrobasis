# Theme 6 Scout Report — Domestic Investing Pressures (Canada)
**Filing day:** 12 Aug 2026 | **Window:** 7–12 Aug 2026

## Thesis
Canadian policy is actively steering pension capital toward domestic assets (30% cap removal, Canada Strong Fund, quarterly ministerial round-tables, airport/utility ownership exploration). Best opportunities align with the domestic-resilience push (energy, AI infra, critical minerals, defence). Risk: politically-driven, non-optimal allocation.

## Divergences / tensions worth flagging to the Evaluator
1. **CPP Investments' Brazil hotel JV (in-window, dated) directly cuts against the thesis** — the country's largest pension pool kept deploying capital abroad inside the exact window Ottawa is pushing domestic reallocation, with no offsetting in-window domestic mega-deal surfaced to balance it.
2. **TSX record highs (in-window) are oil/geopolitics-driven, not policy-driven** — attributing the rally to the domestic-investment push would overstate the thesis; the proximate cause (Iran-Hormuz tension lifting oil) belongs to Theme 5, and conflating the two would be a materiality/causality error in the Theme 6 write-up.
3. **Structural backdrop (pre-window but load-bearing)**: foreign assets are still out-earning Canadian ones (Q2 global equities +15.1% vs Canada bonds +2.0%) and PE — a preferred vehicle for the domestic push — is underperforming public markets on Hamilton Lane/State Street data. Both remain the strongest fundamental counterarguments to the "invest-at-home" thesis, even though the specific data releases predate this window.
